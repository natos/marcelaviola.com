Pregnancy and the months after birth change what your body needs from training. Marcela is a Girls Gone Strong Certified Pre & Postnatal Coach, and adapts strength and mobility work to where you are — before birth, and as you return to training afterwards.

As with every client, it starts with understanding you: your history, how you feel, and what matters most to you right now. Always check with your doctor or midwife before starting.
