If you are tired of that nagging lower back pain, you keep getting injured in the same spot, or you want to take your athletic performance to the next level, it's time to start working on your mobility.

Mobility training combines different methods to improve your full range of motion and your capacity to move through it with control and awareness.

Having proper mobility requires much more than stretching. You'll train your flexibility together with the strength needed to support your muscles and joints — the same approach Marcela has refined over 20 years as an aerialist, yoga teacher and handstand practitioner.
