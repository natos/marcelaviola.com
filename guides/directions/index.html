Photo to shoot Goblet squat, bottom position. Side-on, plain background, weight at the chest.

How to: goblet squat

  1. Hold the weight at your chest, elbows pointing down.
  2. Feet just wider than hips, toes slightly out.
  3. Sit down between your heels, chest tall.
  4. Push the floor away to stand. Breathe out on the way up.

How hard should it feel?

Train at a 6 to 8 out of 10

6–8

Finish each set with 2 to 4 reps still in the tank. Hard enough to change, easy enough to repeat.

If your form breaks, the set is over. That rep doesn't count, even if you can force it up.

Progress you can see

Your goblet squat, week by week

Add reps first, then weight. Small steps each week add up to a big change in a month.

Write it down. A note on your phone with the date, weight and reps is all the tracking you need.
