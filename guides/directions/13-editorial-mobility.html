Why mobility matters

Stiffness is a habit you can undo

Hours at a desk teach the hips and upper back to stay short and still. A few minutes of gentle stretching every day teaches them the opposite.

Daily routine · 10 min

Five moves for a looser body

  1. Cat–cow10 slow rounds
  2. Kneeling hip flexor stretch2 × 30 s / side
  3. Standing forward fold2 × 30 s
  4. Open book8 / side
  5. Deep squat hold2 × 30 s

How to: standing forward fold

  1. Stand with feet hip-width apart, knees soft.
  2. Hinge at the hips and let your upper body hang.
  3. Bend your knees as much as you need to reach.
  4. Let your head go heavy. Breathe out to sink a little lower.

03

How far should you go?

Stretch to a 4 to 6 out of 10

4–6

A clear pull you can breathe through. If you have to hold your breath, back off a little.

Stretching should never hurt. Sharp or pinching pain means stop and come out of the position slowly.

04

Progress you can feel

Build up to 60 seconds a stretch

Start with short holds and add five seconds each week. By week four, each stretch gets its full minute.

Same time, same place. Stretch after brushing your teeth or before bed. Linking it to a habit makes it stick.
