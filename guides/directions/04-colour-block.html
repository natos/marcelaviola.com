01 · Why strength mattersTraining

Muscle is your long-term health investment

From around our 30s, muscle and strength start to decline, unless we give the body a reason to keep them. Strength training is that reason.

Action planWeek 1

Your first week

  • Pick three days and block 40 minutes in your calendar
  • Find a dumbbell or kettlebell. 4–8 kg is a good start
  • Clear a space of about 2 × 2 m
  • Do Session A once, slowly, and note how it felt
  • Rest at least one day between sessions
