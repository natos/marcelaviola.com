Why strength matters

Muscle is your long-term health investment

From around our 30s, muscle and strength start to decline, unless we give the body a reason to keep them. Strength training is that reason.

2+

Days a week. The WHO recommends muscle-strengthening activity for all major muscle groups on two or more days a week.

Start lighter than you thinkWeeks one and two are about learning each movement well. The weight comes later.

Session A · 40 min

Your first full-body session

  1. Goblet squat3 × 8 · 90 s
  2. Incline push-up3 × 8 · 60 s
  3. One-arm row3 × 10 / side · 60 s
  4. Glute bridge3 × 12 · 60 s
  5. Dead bug3 × 6 / side · 45 s

How to: goblet squat

  1. Hold the weight at your chest, elbows pointing down.
  2. Feet just wider than hips, toes slightly out.
  3. Sit down between your heels, chest tall.
  4. Push the floor away to stand. Breathe out on the way up.

Action plan

Your first week

  • Pick three days and block 40 minutes in your calendar
  • Find a dumbbell or kettlebell. 4–8 kg is a good start
  • Clear a space of about 2 × 2 m
  • Do Session A once, slowly, and note how it felt
  • Rest at least one day between sessions
